interJEV Explorer cannot display the webpage

Most likely causes:

  • You are not connected to JEV.
  • OPENROUTER_API_KEY or JEV_MODEL is missing or wrong in your .env.
  • JEV is still imagining this part of the internet.

What you can try:

  • Check your .env and restart the server.
  • Run with JEV_MOCK=1 to browse offline.
  • Return to the interJEV home page.
